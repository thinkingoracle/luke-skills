# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

This catalog exists so that people can extend a personal assistant safely. That
only works if contributing here is a good experience for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how they found us.

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for all participants.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person.
- Assume a proposal was made in good faith. Most boundary violations are
  misunderstandings of a deliberately narrow policy, not attempts to evade it.
- When you decline something, say which rule and why, and point at the document.
  "Out of boundary" without a reason is not review.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on eligibility, and that the call
  is about the proposal, not the person.
- Give first-time contributors the benefit of the doubt. The V1 boundary is
  unusual and the review flow is unusual; confusion is expected.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate derailment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting content designed to deceive a reviewer or a Luke user. That
  includes concealed instructions, misleading descriptions, or authority a
  skill does not declare. This is a conduct matter as well as a security one.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass the boundary, the review lifecycle, or the
  trust model.
- Disclosing a suspected vulnerability publicly instead of through
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, and skill content itself, and when representing
the project publicly.

## Reporting

Email <support@thinkingoracle.com>. Reports go to the Luke Support desk, which
owns intake and routes each report to an assigned responder, so a report about
one person is never handled only by that person.

Reports are handled confidentially. The reporter's identity is not shared with
the reported party.

Do not use a public issue to report a conduct concern about a specific person,
and do not use the private vulnerability channel for one. That channel is for
suspected security vulnerabilities only, and it reaches a different queue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code, and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Enforcement aims to be proportionate: a correction and an explanation first, a
restriction only when a pattern continues or the conduct is severe.
